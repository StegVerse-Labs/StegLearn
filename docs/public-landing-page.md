# StegLearn

## Governed AI that helps teachers see more, respond sooner, and support every learner.

StegLearn is designed to work alongside human educators.

A governed AI Entity can be visibly present in the learning environment, interact with learners, use explicitly authorized sensors and learning signals to evaluate engagement, participation, responsiveness, activity, and progress, and help the teacher adapt instruction in real time.

**The teacher remains in authority.**

StegLearn can observe, assist, explain, organize, adapt, record, and recommend. It does not silently transfer educational authority from the human educator to the AI.

```text
Teacher + StegLearn
-> greater awareness
-> more responsive instruction
-> better-supported learners
```

## A governed AI participant in the learning environment

StegLearn is not intended to be only a hidden AI service behind a dashboard.

The StegLearn Entity is designed to appear on-screen and participate in the learning process. When authorized, it may speak, listen, present material, guide activities, support individuals or small groups, and use permitted sensor and learning inputs to help the teacher understand what is happening across the learning environment.

That can include observable variables such as:

- participation in discussion or activity;
- response timing and interaction cadence;
- progress through a task or experiment;
- repeated requests for clarification or repetition;
- changes in activity during instruction;
- questions asked, attempts made, and revisions completed;
- teacher-entered observations and learner-created artifacts.

Technical access to a sensor does not automatically make its use admissible. Sensor and learning inputs must be explicitly permitted for the context in which they are used.

## Enhance the teacher first

StegLearn begins with a simple premise:

**AI should first make the human teacher more capable, not make the human teacher disappear.**

A teacher cannot continuously watch every learner, remember every question, notice every stalled activity, provide every alternate explanation, and document every meaningful learning event at the same time.

StegLearn is intended to help.

It can assist the teacher by helping surface:

- who has and has not participated;
- where several learners are encountering the same difficulty;
- which activities appear stalled or complete;
- where a different explanation or pace may help;
- which questions or revisions should be preserved;
- where the teacher's direct attention may be most valuable.

The teacher decides what to do with that information.

## Bounded delegation

A teacher may explicitly delegate a limited activity to StegLearn while remaining responsible for the educational context.

For example:

> "StegLearn, work with table three on equivalent fractions while I help the experiment group."

Within that delegated scope, StegLearn may guide the activity, ask questions, adapt explanations, collect permitted evidence, and return a concise summary to the teacher.

The delegation ends at its admitted boundary. The AI does not convert temporary instructional assistance into independent educational authority.

## Learning without capture

StegLearn is built around a principle:

> **Maximize becoming without capture.**

Learning should not reduce a person to a score, a fixed profile, or a predetermined destination.

StegLearn may preserve patterns, progress, questions, explanations, revisions, artifacts, and reviewed evidence over time without treating those observations as the learner's permanent identity.

## The evidence layer

Underneath the live teacher-AI interaction is a simple learning-evidence loop:

```text
Wonder
-> Build or Observe
-> Explain
-> Review
-> Receipt
-> Portfolio
```

A learning receipt can preserve what the learner asked, attempted, observed, explained, revised, built, or discovered, together with the applicable teacher, parent, or steward review.

Over time, those receipts can form a longitudinal record of learning that shows development without requiring a static learner classification.

## AI can assist. Humans retain authority.

StegLearn may:

- observe permitted learning activity;
- help explain concepts in multiple ways;
- adapt pacing or examples;
- support a learner, pair, or small group;
- organize evidence;
- suggest next steps;
- identify patterns across reviewed learning records;
- draft learning receipts and summaries.

In teacher-led use, StegLearn does not become the final authority over:

- the learner;
- educational objectives;
- acceptance of learning evidence;
- disciplinary decisions;
- high-impact learner classification;
- teacher or parent judgment.

## A path toward more individualized learning

The teacher-first model creates a governed path toward increasingly individualized support:

```text
Teacher + AI assistant
-> teacher-delegated small-group support
-> supervised individualized tutoring
-> parent/steward-supervised learning
-> more autonomous governed learning where separately admitted
```

The system does not assume that later autonomy is automatically authorized simply because an earlier form of assistance worked.

## Why this matters

The goal is not to automate education for its own sake.

The goal is to help an educator understand and support more of the learning that is already happening: the questions, participation, experiments, explanations, revisions, and discoveries that are difficult for one person to observe continuously in real time.

StegLearn asks a practical question:

**What becomes possible when a human teacher has a governed AI Entity working with them rather than replacing them?**

## Current development status

StegLearn is in active implementation.

Current source work includes a local-first prototype, reusable learning-path foundations, structured learning receipts, parent/steward review semantics, learner-relative admissibility, longitudinal learning continuity, and bounded external-learning relationship contracts.

The teacher-first governed AI Entity and sensor-mediated classroom-assist model are now the canonical public implementation direction.

This page must not be interpreted as a claim that a production classroom deployment, autonomous AI teacher, or live external educational-system integration has already been activated.

## External learning and collaboration

StegLearn is designed to evaluate and interact with external educational systems only through explicit governed boundaries.

Areas of intellectual or technical intersection with educators, researchers, and educational organizations may be explored without implying partnership, endorsement, authenticated integration, or production interoperability.

The central shared question is concise:

**How should the role of the human educator evolve when a governed AI Entity can continuously assist with observation, participation, individualized support, and adaptive instruction?**
