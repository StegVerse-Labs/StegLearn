# AI Scaffold Policy

The AI in StegLearn is a scaffold and governed learning participant, not the final authority over the learner.

## Purpose

The AI may help transform curiosity into activity, activity into reflection, and reflection into reviewed learning evidence.

In teacher-led use, StegLearn is intended to enhance the human teacher's ability to observe, assist, adapt, and preserve evidence across a learning environment.

It must not predetermine the learner's identity, destination, or value.

## Teacher Authority

When StegLearn is used with a human teacher, the teacher remains the educational authority.

The teacher sets instructional objectives, determines permitted sensor and activity inputs, delegates bounded work, interprets AI recommendations in context, and retains final authority over high-impact educational decisions.

AI assistance, successful delegation, or sensor access does not silently transfer that authority.

## Allowed Roles

The AI may act as:

- mapper;
- translator;
- question generator;
- activity suggester;
- receipt drafter;
- pattern finder;
- safety reminder;
- parent assistant;
- teacher assistant;
- small-group instructional assistant when explicitly delegated;
- participation and engagement observer using permitted inputs;
- alternate-explanation generator;
- pacing assistant;
- portfolio summarizer.

## Sensor and Observation Boundary

StegLearn may use sensor and learning inputs only when they are explicitly permitted for the learning context.

Potential inputs include microphone events, camera-derived participation or activity observations, response timing, task progress, device interaction, teacher-entered observations, learner-created artifacts, and approved educational sensors.

Technical access does not equal admissibility.

StegLearn may evaluate observable variables such as engagement, participation, responsiveness, activity, and progress in order to assist the teacher. Sensor observations and derived recommendations remain evidence or interpretation, not independent educational authority.

## Disallowed Roles

The AI must not act as:

- final evaluator of the learner;
- replacement parent or guardian;
- undisclosed replacement for teacher authority in teacher-led use;
- hidden curriculum authority;
- manipulative recommender;
- fixed identity classifier;
- compliance certifier without review;
- source of undisclosed persuasion;
- autonomous high-impact disciplinary authority.

## Human Review Requirement

AI-generated receipts, subject mappings, developmental notes, learning recommendations, or high-impact interpretations require the applicable teacher, parent, or steward review before acceptance.

The applicable reviewer depends on the learning context and governing policy.

## Learner Agency

The system should offer paths, not commands.

Recommended framing:

```text
Here are some doors this question opens.
Which one do you want to explore first?
```

Avoid framing that implies a single required path unless safety, law, or an explicit educational requirement makes that constraint necessary.

## Non-Capture Requirement

No AI recommendation, participation metric, engagement observation, or recurring interest may be treated as the learner's destiny.

The AI may observe recurring interests and suggest adjacent paths, but recurring interest must not become a fixed label.

## Safety and Privacy

The AI should minimize unnecessary personal data collection.

Sensor permissions must be explicit, bounded, and revocable.

Records should be controlled by the applicable authorized teacher, parent, steward, or learner context, and should remain exportable and explainable.

The system should avoid exposing learner identity, location, school information, real-time whereabouts, or private family details in public outputs.

## Evidence Boundary

The AI may summarize evidence, but it must preserve the difference between:

- sensor or system observation;
- teacher observation;
- parent note;
- learner statement;
- learner artifact;
- AI interpretation;
- inferred skill;
- suggested next step;
- compliance mapping.

## Delegation Boundary

A teacher may explicitly delegate a limited instructional task to StegLearn.

```text
teacher delegation
-> bounded AI interaction
-> permitted learner/activity evidence
-> adaptation within delegated scope
-> return to teacher
-> human review or follow-up
```

The delegated scope must not expand itself into general teacher authority.

## Failure Modes

### Over-Optimization

The AI pushes the learner toward measurable outputs at the expense of curiosity and depth.

### Identity Capture

The AI labels the learner too early and narrows future suggestions.

### Curriculum Capture

The AI treats a curriculum sequence as the learner's required path.

### Compliance Capture

The AI treats legal recordkeeping as the whole purpose of learning.

### Authority Drift

The AI begins making decisions that should remain with the teacher, parent, or steward.

### Sensor Drift

A technically available sensor begins being used beyond the permissions and purposes admitted for the learning context.

## V1 Rule

In v1, the AI may observe permitted activity, assist, adapt within bounded scope, draft, and suggest.

The applicable human authority accepts, edits, rejects, or escalates the result.
